# anti patterns
Reject speculative generality, middle men, god objects, shotgun surgery, primitive obsession, interface/factory explosions, premature optimization, overfragmentation, pattern abuse, redundant defensive checks and reinventing existing functionality. Review each new class, interface, file, folder, dependency, abstraction and configuration for a distinct responsibility or confirmed need. Small related improvements allowed; no unauthorized major refactoring. Preserve transactions, invariants, concurrency control, idempotency, authorization, precision and meaningful tests. Do not deduplicate semantically distinct rules.
